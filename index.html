Our Story

Brew & Byte was born in 2019 from a simple idea: every great hackathon deserves great coffee. We source single-origin beans from ethical farms, roast them in-house, and pair them with freshly baked pastries — all inside a welcoming space built for makers, thinkers, and caffeine enthusiasts.

Whether you're debugging a tricky function or catching up with a friend, our baristas are ready to fuel your session.
